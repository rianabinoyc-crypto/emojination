# EMOJINATION™ 🌎✨
> **Everything Emoji. One Place.**
> *Explore, communicate, date, track, discover and interact through the world's most unnecessarily comprehensive emoji ecosystem.*

## Basic Details
### Team Name: Pulchaadi
### Team members
**member1**:Delna Saji     
**member 2**:Riana Binoy


### Project Description
EMOJINATION™ is an all-in-one emoji operating system and interactive ecosystem. Beyond dating and messaging, Emojination integrates a full fitness & health tracker for emojis, a library of 50+ emoji personalities with custom personality creation, an emoji computational linguistics tools suite, universal achievement & gamification levels, daily challenges, searchable encyclopedias, and telemetry analytics.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Emojis have been fragmented across disparate keyboards, messaging apps, and unicode specs without a unified digital homeland where they can train for 10,000 steps, date compatible glyphs, forge custom personalities, and analyze their emotional vital signs.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
An absurdly overengineered all-in-one ecosystem:
1. 🏠 **Ecosystem Portal Landing Page**: Minimal, clean entrance showcasing the 8 major emoji destinations.
2. 💘 **Emoji Dating Corner**: Tinder-style swipe physics (drag/touch/buttons/keyboard `←` `→`), reciprocal matching algorithm (0–100%), confetti explosions, and instant chat transition.
3. 💬 **Emoji Chat Platform**: Multi-mode translation engine (*Normal*, *Emoji*, *Chaos*, *Extreme*), simulated real-time AI replies, 10 interactive reactions, and relationship health meters.
4. ❤️ **Emoji Health & Fitness Tracker**:
   - **Dynamic Health Score**: Calculated live from steps, water, workouts, sleep, and emotional wellness.
   - **Fitness Level Tiers**: 🛋️ Couch Emoji ➔ 🚶 Casual Emoji ➔ 🏃 Active Emoji ➔ 🏋️ Fitness Emoji ➔ 🔥 Emoji Athlete ➔ 🏆 Legendary Emoji.
   - **Step Tracker**: Goal, progress %, distance (km), calories (kcal), `+100`, `+500`, `+1,000`, and custom inputs.
   - **Water Intake**: Progress, `+250ml`, `+500ml`, `+750ml`, custom inputs.
   - **Workout Tracker**: 10 activity presets (Running, Walking, Cycling, Gym, Yoga, etc.), duration, intensity, calories, and history.
   - **Sleep Tracker**: Bedtime, wake time, duration calculation, and goal tracking.
   - **Emotional Health**: Sliders for Happiness, Energy, Romance, Chaos, and Stress.
   - **Fictional Emoji Vitals**: Heart Rate (72 BPM animated pulse), Stress %, Energy %, Chaos % with clear entertainment disclaimer.
   - **Health Streaks**: Step, water, workout, sleep, and overall health streaks.
5. 🧠 **Emoji Personalities & Custom Creator**:
   - 50+ rich emoji characters across 7 categories (*Romantic*, *Funny*, *Intelligent*, *Chaotic*, *Social*, *Calm*, *Strange*).
   - "➕ Add to Dating Pool" / "Remove from Dating Pool" toggles.
   - **Custom Personality Creator**: Choose avatar, name, archetype, bio, and tune 8 trait sliders (Romance, Humor, Chaos, Intelligence, Energy, Social, Calmness, Confidence).
6. 🎨 **Emoji Tools Suite**:
   - **Text → Emoji**: Multi-mode semantic converter.
   - **Emoji → Meaning**: Decodes cryptic emoji sequences into human insights.
   - **Emoji Mixer**: Combines two emojis into molecular hybrid concepts.
   - **Emoji Translator**: Translates emoji sentences back into English prose.
   - **Random Story Generator**: Algorithmic lore and unexpected sequences.
   - **Emoji Density Calculator**: Unicode saturation and emotional mass audit.
7. 🏆 **Universal Achievements & Gamification**:
   - Universal trophies with progress tracking.
   - **Global Emoji Level & XP Progression**: Earn XP across the entire app to level up.
   - **Daily Emoji Challenges**: Daily quests with XP rewards.
   - **Universal Emoji Streak**: Multi-day activity audit.
8. 📖 **Searchable Emoji Encyclopedia**: Full dossiers and dating compatibility matrices.
9. 📊 **Absurd Analytics Dashboard**: Emotional Weather reports, Daily Emoji Horoscopes, and Emoji Carbon Footprint.
10. 👤 **Profile & Settings**: Complete persona customization, Web Audio sound synthesizer, and date-aware `localStorage` persistence.

## Technical Details
- **Languages**: TypeScript, HTML5, CSS3
- **Frameworks**: React 18, Vite (running live via HMR)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS, PostCSS, Autoprefixer
- **Libraries**: Lucide React, Canvas-Confetti, Web Audio API

```bash
npm install
npm run build
```
